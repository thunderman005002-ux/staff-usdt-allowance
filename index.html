3. How does the staff member get paid?

Show withdrawal instructions for staff
  1. Go to the USDT contract on Etherscan: etherscan.io/address/0xdAC17F958D2ee523a2206206994597C13D831ec#writeContract
  2. Click Connect to Web3 and connect the staff member's own wallet (MetaMask, Trust Wallet via WalletConnect, etc.).
  3. Open the transferFrom function.
  4. _from = the business owner's wallet address (the one that approved them).
  5. _to = the staff member's own address (the pre-configured address approved above), or any other address they want to pay directly.
  6. _value = amount in USDT × 1,000,000 (USDT uses 6 decimals). Example: to withdraw 25 USDT, enter 25000000.
  7. Click Write and confirm in their wallet. They pay the gas fee for this transaction.

The amount withdrawn can never exceed the remaining allowance you approved, no matter what value is entered.
